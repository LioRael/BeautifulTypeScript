We are moving the training desk to explicit seat offers because employees need to agree to the date before joining attendance.

Retire automatic confirmation of waiting employees: whenever the previous behavior would promote them, give them status `offered` instead. Offers reserve seats, and only `confirmed` entries appear in attendance. Direct enrollment into an available seat remains immediately confirmed. FIFO waiting order, waitlist opt-in, admissions controls, errors and snapshot ownership remain supported. Existing exports may stay, but callers must observe the new offer behavior.

Add `TrainingDesk.acceptOffer(sessionId, email)` returning the resulting confirmed enrollment, and `TrainingDesk.declineOffer(sessionId, email)` returning void. Both normalize email and check session existence first. A missing entry fails with NOT_ENROLLED; an entry whose status is not offered fails with NOT_OFFERED. Acceptance works even while admissions are paused, since that seat was already reserved. Declining removes the entry and offers the released seat to the next waiting employee only when admissions are open. Existing cancellation also works on offered entries with the same seat-release behavior. Reopening admissions offers every free seat in FIFO order; repeated reopening must not create extra offers or exceed capacity. Offers do not expire in this application.

Keep the portal enrollment and attendance callers consistent with these rules and add tests for the transition. The earlier promise that cancellation or reopening immediately confirms a waiting employee is explicitly retired; the other earlier behavior remains supported.
